Sn Secure Notes KNOW YOUR NOTEBOOK

Privacy, with clear limits.

Your account separates your notes from other accounts. This classroom app runs on your computer and is intended for sample data.

Access belongs to you

Every note request checks the signed-in account. Signing out revokes the session. Sessions expire after 30 minutes.

Your words stay words

Notes are plain text. HTML is displayed as text, and forms are checked before changes are accepted.

Passwords are hashed

Passwords use a salted, slow hash. Repeated sign-in and registration attempts are limited.

Use sample notes

Note content is stored without encryption. Someone with access to the database or computer can read it. This is not an encrypted vault.

Before broader use

The local demo uses HTTP on loopback. Public use would need HTTPS, a production server, operational monitoring, backups, account recovery, and a security review. The app has no MFA, sharing, attachments, or password reset. Deleted notes may remain in database pages or backups.
