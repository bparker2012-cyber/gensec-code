# Harbor Health Incident Response Handbook

## Severity levels

A Severity 1 incident is an active compromise, ransomware event, or outage
affecting patient care. The incident commander must page the security director
within 10 minutes and notify the privacy officer within 30 minutes.

A Severity 2 incident is suspicious activity with limited impact. The on-call
analyst must begin triage within one hour and preserve relevant logs.

## Evidence retention

Firewall, identity, and endpoint logs related to an incident are retained for
365 days. Investigators store forensic images in the restricted evidence vault
and record a SHA-256 checksum in the case log.

## Recovery

Systems return to service only after the incident commander documents
containment, the system owner validates critical functions, and Security
completes a final malware scan.
