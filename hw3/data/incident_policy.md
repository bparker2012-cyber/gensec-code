# Classroom incident response policy

This is fictional classroom policy, not organizational or legal guidance.

## Severity 1
Confirmed data exfiltration or ransomware is Severity 1. Notify the incident commander immediately and the privacy officer within 30 minutes. Isolate affected hosts while preserving evidence. Record UTC timestamps and maintain chain of custody.

## Severity 2
An outage without confirmed data loss is Severity 2. Notify the on-call responder within 60 minutes. Collect service logs and scope affected systems before changing configuration.

## Severity 3
A suspicious event without confirmed impact is Severity 3. Open a tracking ticket and investigate within one business day. Preserve the original alert.
